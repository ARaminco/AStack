# Risk Register

## Scoring
Score = probability (1-5) x impact (1-5). Bands: low <= 3, medium 4-8, high 9-15, critical >= 16.

## Register
| Id | Risk | P | I | Score | Band | Response | Owner | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Responses
avoid removes the cause, mitigate reduces probability or impact, transfer moves it to a third party, accept records it consciously.

## Review Cadence
Re-score open risks at every sprint close and before every stage gate.
